What are Study Groups?

Study groups are collaborative learning sessions where students can come together to review material, solve problems, and prepare for exams.

Benefits of Joining a Study Group:

  • Enhanced understanding of course material
  • Improved academic performance
  • Social interaction and networking with peers

The Process:

To ensure that students find a right match, they will be assigned study groups with other alumni within the same range of courses. Students can either enter their major or the courses they are taking in order to get a more precise match in regarads to study groups. To schedule a study group meeting, navigate to the "Join NCAT Study Groups" page and fill out the registration form.
