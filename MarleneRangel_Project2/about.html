Welcome to the NCAT Study Group Service

The NCAT Study Group Service is designed to help students connect and collaborate in their studies. Our mission is to enhance the academic experience by providing an organized platform for study groups.

At NCAT, we believe that learning together fosters a stronger understanding of course material and builds a supportive community. A large part of the Aggie Student Body includes students who are online students and not present on campus. It is understandable that it may be difficult as a student to enter the learning space by themselves. Additionally, it can be challenging to become accustomed to online learning from a different location. Therefore, NCAT wants to introduce a way for students to be able to help eachother and conncet. This not only applies to online students though, and our Aggies on campus are welcome to join as well. NCAT Study Group is a safe place where students can connect with eachother based on what courses they are taking and talk about topics that relate to their courses in which they need help. There are also options to be totally anonymous if the student so wishes!
